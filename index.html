Interactive preview

Try the four tree layouts

Move people, pan and zoom a fictional family tree. Each of the four views keeps its own arrangement while you explore.

The preview shows three generations. In the app, you can add earlier ancestors and later descendants, with up to 5,000 people per tree.

Features

Build, organize and share

  • Give each person a profile

    Add names, a portrait and memories. Record a full date or just a year when that is all you know.

  • See the whole family

    Record parents, children and partnerships, including separations and divorces. Switch between Classic, Horizontal, Tapestry and Chart.

  • Keep trees on your device

    Manage separate trees with their own backup histories. Export a PDF when you want to print or share a picture of the tree.

  • Share a tree with a password

    Export profiles, photos and relationships in a password-protected .urinamu file. The recipient uses the same password to import the file as a separate tree in the desktop app.

  • Use the app in your language

    Choose German, English or Korean in the app. Japanese is available starting with UriNamu 0.10.0.
