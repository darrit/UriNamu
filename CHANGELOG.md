# Changelog

## 0.6.2 — 2026-10-27

- The PDF libraries load only when a PDF is exported, so the built application stays under Vite's chunk-size warning.

## 0.6.1 — 2026-10-27

- Export asks whether to write an ordinary PDF or an encrypted UriNamu file. The PDF is a normal picture of the open tree, no longer a large high-resolution poster.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-10-27

- A partnership can store a wedding date. It is entered with the marital status, shown in the profile, and drawn on the tree beside the couple.

## 0.5.0 — 2026-10-27

- A PDF export writes the open family tree as a large, sharp picture for printing. The sheet is twice the on-screen layout at 300 DPI, and a very large tree continues across pages instead of being squeezed onto A4.

## 0.4.52 — 2026-10-27

- Every guide explanation that describes a window or a control now shows that window: the member form, the profile, the menu states, search, dynasty, the library, merge, history and export.

## 0.4.51 — 2026-10-27

- The guide walks through each control beside the real button or picture, and the text names where that control sits and what it does.

## 0.4.50 — 2026-10-27

- The guide menu matches the real menu, and its text names the undo arrow at the top right and the partner-space check in Settings.

## 0.4.49 — 2026-10-27

- Guide pictures are the real cards, ribbons and lines of the Morgenstern example, including the menu, the three views, tidy-up and a dynasty.

## 0.4.48 — 2026-10-27

- A guide in the sidebar explains the first steps and the later possibilities, with small previews. The first hints can be skipped and stay quiet afterwards.

## 0.4.47 — 2026-10-27

- The Windows installer installs the WebView2 runtime when it is missing. An existing runtime stays in place, and the download needs an internet connection.

## 0.4.46 — 2026-10-26

- People who do not belong to a dynasty are pushed beside its area. The area stays clear of them, like a wall.

## 0.4.45 — 2026-10-26

- A dynasty belongs to a person, including someone without a partner. A partner can found a different dynasty or join the one already founded.

## 0.4.44 — 2026-10-26

- Portrait and dynasty pictures may be up to 50 MB. The dynasty dialog states that limit beside the file field. Stored pictures stay scaled down, and a family file remains limited to 32 MB.

## 0.4.43 — 2026-10-26

- A dynasty badge without a picture centers its name inside the corner mark.

## 0.4.42 — 2026-10-26

- The dynasty entry in a person's context menu names that person, not their partner.

## 0.4.41 — 2026-10-26

- A dynasty picture sits once in the center of its area and keeps the uploaded image's proportions. It is no longer tiled or stretched to fill that area.

## 0.4.40 — 2026-10-26

- A couple can start a dynasty. Every descendant belongs to it. Its mark sits in the top-left or top-right corner and repeats as a watermark across that area.

## 0.4.39 — 2026-10-26

- Rename a family tree from My family trees. The open tree updates immediately; other trees keep their own backup history.

## 0.4.38 — 2026-10-26

- Tidy keeps siblings of a merged couple at the normal gap. The in-law ancestor line no longer pushes the birth family a full couple-width away.

## 0.4.37 — 2026-10-26

- Tidy puts an in-law ancestor line beside that partner and keeps cousins under their own parents. The partners switch sides so each one stands toward their own family.

## 0.4.36 — 2026-10-26

- Seat a merged couple beside their siblings, and move the other branch in to the normal gap instead of leaving a wide hole on that row.

## 0.4.35 — 2026-10-26

- When two trees meet at a couple, the shallower family moves down with that partner. Siblings share the couple's row, and their parents sit with the other parents instead of on the oldest generation.

## 0.4.34 — 2026-10-26

- Choose Deutsch, English, or 한국어 on first launch, and change it later in Settings. The whole application follows the selected language.

## 0.4.33 — 2026-10-26

- Accept Apple's xar table-of-contents checksum when installing Command Line Tools. Setup no longer aborts when that catalog digest differs from a hash of the whole package.

## 0.4.32 — 2026-10-26

## 0.4.31 — 2026-10-26

- Show the child plus only while a couple has no child yet and has not been marked childless.
